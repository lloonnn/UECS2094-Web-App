UTAR Library Details

About the Library

The UTAR Library supports the teaching, learning and research activities of the University. It provides a wide collection of printed books, journals, newspapers, audio-visual materials and a growing collection of electronic resources such as e-books, e-journals and online databases. Members may also use the discussion rooms, computer areas and quiet reading zones inside the library.

Library Rules

  • Show your student or staff card when entering the library.
  • Food and drinks are not allowed inside the library.
  • Keep silence in the reading and study areas.
  • Return the borrowed books on or before the due date to avoid a fine.
  • Take care of the library materials and do not write on them.
